Manchester United’s FA Cup campaign came to an early end after a **2-1 home defeat against Brighton & Hove Albion** at Old Trafford. The loss marked United’s **first third-round FA Cup exit since the 2013–14 season**, making it a significant moment in their 2025–26 campaign.

Here’s a simple breakdown of **what happened and why this result is getting attention**.

---

## Early Brighton Goal Sets the Tone

United started the match positively and created early chances through Diogo Dalot and Bruno Fernandes. However, despite controlling possession in the opening minutes, it was Brighton who struck first.

In the **12th minute**, a cross from Danny Welbeck caused problems in the United box. Although Lisandro Martinez initially cleared Georginio Rutter’s header off the line, **Brajan Gruda reacted quickest** and finished from close range to give Brighton the lead.

The goal shifted momentum and forced United to chase the game earlier than expected.

---

## United Control Possession but Lack Finishing

For much of the first half, United dominated the ball and created half-chances. Benjamin Sesko, Matheus Cunha, and Bruno Fernandes all had attempts, but Brighton goalkeeper **Jason Steele** dealt comfortably with most efforts.

Despite the pressure, United went into halftime **1-0 down**, struggling to convert possession into clear-cut opportunities.

---

## Welbeck Doubles Brighton’s Lead

United began the second half with renewed intent, but the match took a decisive turn just after the hour mark.

In the **64th minute**, former United striker **Danny Welbeck** received a pass from Gruda and fired a powerful left-footed shot into the top corner, making it **2-0 to Brighton**. The goal significantly increased the challenge facing the hosts.

---

## Late Fightback and Red Card Drama

United finally found a breakthrough in the **85th minute**, when **Benjamin Sesko headed home** from a Bruno Fernandes corner, giving the home crowd brief hope of a comeback.

However, that momentum was quickly halted. Substitute **Shea Lacey was sent off in the 89th minute** after receiving a second yellow card for dissent, reducing United to ten men during the closing stages.

Despite a late header from Harry Maguire, United were unable to find an equaliser.

---

## Why This FA Cup Exit Matters

This result has drawn attention for several reasons:

- **Early elimination** from a major domestic competition  
- **First FA Cup loss to Brighton** in the competition’s history  
- Continued inconsistency during a transitional phase  
- Interim head coach **Darren Fletcher’s second match in charge**

The defeat places additional pressure on United to deliver results in the Premier League, especially with a Manchester derby approaching.

---

## What’s Next for Manchester United?

United now turn their focus to the **Premier League**, with the **198th Manchester derby against Manchester City** scheduled at Old Trafford. The match will be a key test of character following the FA Cup disappointment.

Manchester United’s FA Cup exit wasn’t about a lack of effort, but rather **missed chances, defensive lapses, and a moment of indiscipline late on**. Brighton executed their game plan efficiently and took their opportunities when it mattered most.
