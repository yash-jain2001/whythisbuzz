## Why Newcastle vs Man City Is Getting Attention

This Carabao Cup semifinal first leg brings together two teams arriving in strong form.

Newcastle, the defending EFL Cup champions, have built momentum with recent domestic wins and will rely heavily on their home crowd at St. James’ Park. Manchester City, meanwhile, continue their dominant run across competitions, extending an unbeaten streak with a high-scoring FA Cup victory.

The contrast between Newcastle’s home advantage and City’s attacking depth has made this fixture one of the most talked-about matches of the week.

## How to Watch Newcastle vs Man City in the US

Viewers in the United States can watch the Carabao Cup semifinal live on **Paramount Plus**.

Paramount Plus offers:
- Full EFL Cup coverage
- Support for mobile, web, and smart TVs
- Multiple subscription plans depending on ad preferences

This is the official broadcaster for the match in the US.

## How to Watch Newcastle vs Man City in the UK for Free

UK viewers have a free option for this match.

- The match can also be streamed online via **ITVX**, ITV’s free streaming platform

Fans with Sky Sports subscriptions can also watch via Sky Sports Main Event or stream through Sky Go or NOW.
