### Why France's Group Winner Status Is Still Up For Grabs

Despite their strong performance, France isn't yet guaranteed to finish as group winners. The reason? Norway, led by the prolific Erling Haaland, also boasts six points in Group I. The group's fate, and therefore potentially France's opponent in the next round, hinges entirely on the direct showdown between these two powerhouses on June 26th at 9 PM (CET).

## Erling Haaland's High-Stakes Challenge: Norway's Goal Difference Dilemma

This is where the plot thickens for Norway and their star striker, Erling Haaland. While they secured a hard-fought 3-2 victory against Senegal, conceding a second goal proved *bitterly* expensive. That single goal means Norway's goal difference is now one goal worse than France's.

**What does this mean for Norway?** It's simple: to snatch the coveted group winner spot and potentially avoid a tougher draw, **Norway *must* defeat France in their final group match.** A draw won't be enough. Had they managed a 3-1 win against Senegal, the higher number of goals scored would have given them the advantage even with a draw against France. Now, it's win-or-bust. This adds an incredible layer of tension and drama to their upcoming fixture.
