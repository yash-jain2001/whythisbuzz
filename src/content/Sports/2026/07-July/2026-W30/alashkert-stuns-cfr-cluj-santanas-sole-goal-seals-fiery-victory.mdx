## Alashkert Stuns CFR Cluj: Rafael Santana's Goal Decides Fiery Encounter

In a match that saw more yellow cards than goals, Alashkert managed to clinch a gritty 1-0 victory over CFR Cluj. The solitary strike from Rafael Santana, beautifully assisted by Karen Nalbandyan, proved to be the decisive moment, leaving CFR Cluj scrambling and Alashkert celebrating a hard-fought win. But this wasn't just about the scoreline; it was about the sheer intensity that permeated every minute on the pitch.

### The Game-Changing Strike: How Alashkert Broke the Deadlock Against CFR Cluj

The tension was palpable, building through a physical encounter until the breakthrough arrived. **Rafael Santana** of Alashkert became the hero of the hour, firing a right-footed shot from the centre of the box straight into the high centre of the goal. The assist, a moment of brilliance, came from **Karen Nalbandyan**, who seemed to be at the heart of much of Alashkert's action – both good and bad, as we'll see. This crucial goal tipped the scales, putting Alashkert ahead and piling pressure on the visitors with just minutes left on the clock, including a mere two minutes of added time.

If goals were scarce, yellow cards were in abundance, painting a picture of a fiercely contested match where neither side was willing to give an inch. The referee had their work cut out for them, dishing out warnings at regular intervals.
