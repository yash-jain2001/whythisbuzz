Eintracht Frankfurt talent Elias Baum was considered a done deal for Hamburger SV, a crucial piece in their summer transfer puzzle. But in a stunning reversal, a new coaching assessment threatens to derail the entire transfer, leaving HSV brass, including Claus Costa, wondering if their top target is now out of reach.

## The Elias Baum Transfer Saga: Why HSV Coveted the Frankfurt Right-Back

For weeks, Elias Baum's name has topped HSV's summer wishlist. The 21-year-old Eintracht Frankfurt professional was seen as the ideal solution to bolster Hamburg's right flank. After a disappointing season with only five Bundesliga appearances and a mere 71 minutes on the pitch, Baum himself was reportedly eager for a new challenge, with HSV emerging as his preferred destination.

Sources indicate HSV had even tabled a concrete offer of around **three million Euros** for Baum, which Frankfurt ultimately rejected. Despite the initial setback, both clubs seemed to be closing in on an agreement, setting the stage for what many believed would be a straightforward transfer. So, what happened?

### Adi Hütter's Impact: How a New Coach Changed Everything

The dramatic twist comes courtesy of Frankfurt's new head coach, Adi Hütter. Following the initial training sessions, Hütter is reportedly so impressed by Baum's abilities that he has frozen the talent's potential departure. More significantly, the planned acquisition of Anton Gaaei from Ajax Amsterdam, another player earmarked to strengthen Frankfurt's right side, has also been put on hold. Why? Because Baum is now considered a serious internal alternative.

This changes everything. A new coach often brings a fresh perspective, and Hütter's assessment has completely reshaped Frankfurt's plans, transforming a player on the fringes into a potential key figure. This unexpected endorsement from the new manager has sent shockwaves through the transfer market and, more specifically, through HSV's recruitment strategy.

## Elias Baum's Future: What Does This Mean for the Player?

For Elias Baum, the situation is complex. After a season where he didn't secure the playing time he'd hoped for following his move from Elversberg, the 32-cap junior international is craving clarity. He's reportedly monitoring the developments with a mix of anticipation and skepticism, awaiting a clear statement from Eintracht Frankfurt on his role, whether that's an immediate move or a renewed commitment. The ambiguity, while potentially offering a lifeline at Frankfurt, also delays his career progression.

### HSV's Dilemma: Scrambling for Alternatives in the Transfer Window

The sudden U-turn is a significant blow to HSV, who had patiently pursued Baum for weeks. Their patience might not pay off. While Baum remains Hamburg's top preference for the right-back role, the club's decision-makers are now actively exploring other options, clearly alarmed by the latest developments from Frankfurt.

This isn't HSV's only headache. The club is actively seeking to reinforce other critical areas, including:

*   **Two new attackers:** To boost their goal-scoring prowess.
*   **A new central defender:** To shore up their backline.
*   **A creative playmaker:** With hopes still high to secure Fábio Vieira from FC Arsenal.

So far, HSV has completed three significant signings this window:

*   **Kofi Amoako** (from Dresden, ~€2 million)
*   **Albert Grønbæk** (from Rennes, €4.7 million)
*   **Martin Adeline** (from Troyes, €4 million)

## What's Next for Elias Baum and the Frankfurt-HSV Transfer Saga?

The resolution of the Elias Baum transfer saga now hinges on Eintracht Frankfurt's pre-season training camp. From July 23rd to August 1st, the Eagles will be based in Grassau, Bavaria. It's during this period that Adi Hütter is expected to make a final decision on Baum's future. Internally, sources suggest that anything is possible – from a complete departure to an early extension of his contract, which currently runs until 2028.

For HSV, this means remaining in a tense holding pattern, with their eyes firmly fixed on Frankfurt. The coming weeks will reveal whether their long pursuit of Elias Baum ends in triumph, or if they'll be forced to pivot decisively to an alternative. The beautiful game's transfer window rarely fails to deliver drama, and this particular saga is shaping up to be one of the summer's most compelling.
