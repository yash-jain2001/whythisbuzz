## World Cup 2026: Argentina Survives Swiss Scare, Marches to Semi-Finals

Kansas City, USA – The defending champions, Argentina, have punched their ticket to the 2026 World Cup semi-finals, but not without a heart-stopping battle against a tenacious Switzerland. In a dramatic quarter-final clash held in Kansas City, the Albiceleste ultimately prevailed 3-1 after extra time, setting up a truly epic encounter with England.

This wasn't a walk in the park for Lionel Messi's squad. The Swiss, despite being reduced to ten men for a significant portion of the match, pushed Argentina to their absolute limits, proving why every game in the World Cup knockout stage is a war.

### How Argentina Broke the Deadlock and Switzerland Fought Back

The match ignited early, with Argentina looking to assert their dominance. It was none other than the maestro, Lionel Messi, who set the stage, delivering a pinpoint corner kick in the 10th minute. Rising above the Swiss defense, Alexis Mac Allister connected with a powerful header, giving Argentina the crucial early lead. The champions seemed to be in control.

However, Switzerland, known for their resilience, refused to fold. They battled back fiercely, and their persistence paid off in the 67th minute when Dan Ndoye netted the equalizer, stunning the Argentinian faithful and pushing the game into a nervy final phase of regular time.

### VAR Controversy: Switzerland's Embolo Sees Red for Simulation

The game took a controversial turn that significantly impacted its trajectory. In the 72nd minute, Swiss forward Breel Embolo found himself in the spotlight for all the wrong reasons. After a tackle, Embolo went down in the box, prompting a review by the Portuguese referee João Pinheiro, who consulted the Video Assistant Referee (VAR).

Following the VAR check, Embolo was controversially shown a second yellow card for simulation, having received his first in the 44th minute. This decision left Switzerland with ten players on the field, facing the daunting task of holding off the reigning champions with a man down for the remainder of the game and potential extra time. While the call was hotly debated, it undeniably shifted the momentum, adding another layer of drama to an already tense encounter.

### Extra Time Heroics: Álvarez and Martínez Seal Argentina's Fate

With the score locked at 1-1 after 90 minutes, the match headed into extra time. Despite their numerical disadvantage, Switzerland continued to defend gallantly, frustrating Argentina's attempts to find a winner. But the champions, driven by the pressure and their star power, eventually broke through.

In the 112th minute, Julián Álvarez found the back of the net, giving Argentina a hard-fought 2-1 lead and sending a wave of relief through their camp. The final nail in the coffin came deep into stoppage time of extra time, with Lautaro Martínez, coming off the bench, scoring in the 120+1 minute, sealing the 3-1 victory and cementing Argentina's place in the final four.

## The World Cup Semi-Finals: England vs. Argentina – A Clash of Titans

With this hard-earned victory, Argentina now prepares for a monumental semi-final showdown. Their next opponent? None other than England, with the match scheduled for Wednesday in Atlanta. This encounter is steeped in historical rivalry and promises to be an explosive contest, truly a World Cup semi-final for the ages.

Meanwhile, the other semi-final fixture will see France take on Spain in Dallas, ensuring two absolutely blockbuster matches that will define the path to the 2026 World Cup final.
