### Brazil's Tactical Shake-Up: Martinelli Steps In for Crucial World Cup Clash Against Norway

The stakes couldn't be higher as Brazil prepares to face Norway in the World Cup Round of 16 this Sunday. But beyond the knockout pressure, all eyes are on Coach Carlo Ancelotti's starting XI, which looks set for a significant, forced change: **Gabriel Martinelli is poised to replace the injured Lucas Paquetá.**

The Arsenal attacker, fresh off scoring the winning goal against Japan in the previous round, has been a consistent starter in recent training sessions. This indicates Ancelotti's leaning towards the dynamic forward to fill the void left by Paquetá, who suffered a left thigh injury rendering him unlikely to play any further part in this World Cup campaign, even if not formally cut from the squad. The critical clash kicks off at 5 PM (Brasília time) at the New York / New Jersey stadium.

### Ancelotti's Conundrum: How Martinelli Fills Paquetá's Big Shoes

The decision to start Martinelli is a testament to his recent form and versatility. While primarily an attacker, sources close to the training camp suggest he'll operate in a slightly deeper role, drifting between the left flank and the center of the pitch when Brazil is in possession. This strategic shift is designed to maintain offensive fluidity while adapting to Paquetá's absence, a player renowned for his creative midfield presence.

Martinelli edged out Botafogo's Danilo Santos, who was also tested in the position. Ancelotti, known for keeping his cards close to his chest, is expected to reveal the final lineup to his squad just hours before kick-off, adding to the pre-match tension.

### Raphinha's Road to Recovery: A Glimmer of Hope for Brazil's Quarter-Final Hopes

In more promising news, Barcelona's **Raphinha** is making strides in his recovery from a right thigh injury sustained against Haiti. He joined teammates for warm-ups and even participated in more integrated exercises on Saturday, a significant step forward from his individual training.

While Raphinha isn't expected to feature against Norway, the medical team is working tirelessly to get him match-fit for a potential quarter-final showdown. If Brazil advances, they would face the winner of Mexico vs. England on July 11th in Miami. This offers a tantalizing prospect of Raphinha returning to bolster the squad for a deeper run in the tournament.

### Beyond the Pitch: Celebrity Buzz and Star Power at the World Cup

It's not just the action on the field that's generating buzz. The World Cup has become a magnet for global attention, attracting an array of celebrities and public figures. During training sessions, even stars like Neymar were spotted arriving via golf cart, adding a touch of casual cool to the preparations.

Match day is expected to draw a constellation of stars to the MetLife Stadium. Reports suggest **Jay-Z, Chris Rock, and Jürgen Klopp** are among the high-profile personalities marking their presence at the Brazil vs. Norway game. Brazilian influencer **Virgínia** has also been seen at the stadium with her daughters, highlighting the crossover appeal of the tournament.

### World Cup News Roundup: Controversies, Comebacks, and Tactical Shifts

The global football stage is never short on drama. In other World Cup news, the decision by FIFA to **suspend the red card issued to USA's Balogun** against Bosnia has sparked considerable debate, labeled "scandalous" by some European media. This decision allows the crucial American attacker to play in their Round of 16 match against Belgium, a move that has left the Belgian camp "perplexed." This echoes historical precedents, including the legendary Garrincha, who famously had a red card overturned in 1962, allowing him to play in the World Cup final.

Meanwhile, Spain sees the return of **Nico Williams** to training, making their full 26-player squad available for the first time as they prepare to face Portugal. Portugal's coach Roberto Martínez, fresh off a win against Croatia, expressed confidence that his team is "better than at the beginning of the World Cup," signaling their readiness for the next challenge.

As Brazil gears up for its pivotal clash, the blend of on-field tactical battles and off-field star power ensures this World Cup continues to deliver unforgettable moments. The question now is: can Brazil overcome their injury setback and navigate the Norwegian challenge to keep their World Cup dreams alive?
