## Beyond 90 Minutes: Understanding the Unique Leagues Cup Format and Points System

The Leagues Cup stands apart from many international football tournaments, particularly with its intriguing approach to draws. There are **no ties** in this competition, adding an extra layer of drama to every single match.

### How Leagues Cup Points Are Awarded: More Than Just a Win

*   **Regular Time Victory:** If a team secures a win within the standard 90 minutes, they are awarded **two points**.
*   **Penalty Shootout Win:** Should the game end in a draw after regulation, the winner is decided by a penalty shootout. The team that emerges victorious from the spot-kick lottery also receives **two points**.
*   **Penalty Shootout Loss:** Even the team that loses in a penalty shootout doesn't go home empty-handed; they still clinch **one point** for their efforts.

This unique scoring system means every game has a decisive outcome, pushing teams to fight for every single point – even if it's just from a shootout loss – to boost their standing.

## Path to the Knockouts: Decoding Leagues Cup Advancement and Group Stage Strategy

The group stage of the Leagues Cup is designed to foster cross-league competition and intense strategic play.

### Navigating the Group Stage: What You Need to Know

*   **Three Matches Per Club:** Each team plays a total of three matches during this initial phase.
*   **Cross-League Competition:** Points are accumulated solely from matches against rivals from the *opposing* league (MLS teams against Liga MX teams, and vice-versa).
*   **Independent Standings:** MLS teams and Liga MX teams maintain separate classification tables. This means the top performers from each league are identified independently.
*   **Advancement:** The top four teams from the MLS table and the top four from the Liga MX table will directly advance to the quarterfinals, where the knockout phase truly begins. This format ensures a balanced representation from both leagues in the later stages of the tournament.

For Inter Miami, every point against Monterrey and other Liga MX opponents is crucial for securing one of those coveted top-four spots and making a deep run in the tournament.
