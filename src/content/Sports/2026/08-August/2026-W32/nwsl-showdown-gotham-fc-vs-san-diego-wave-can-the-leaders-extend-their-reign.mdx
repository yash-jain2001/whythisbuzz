## Why This Game Matters: Gotham FC's NWSL Title Aspirations Face San Diego Wave

The NWSL title race is heating up, and all eyes are on Sports Illustrated Stadium this Friday as league leaders Gotham FC host San Diego Wave FC. This isn't just another match; it's a critical clash that could solidify Gotham's grip on the top spot, extending their lead and etching new records into club history. After a successful road swing, the reigning champions are back on home turf, fueled by momentum and key lineup returns.

Kickoff is set for 8 p.m. ET, with Prime Video carrying the national broadcast, ensuring no one misses what promises to be a thrilling encounter.

## Key Returns for Gotham FC: Bruninha & Berger Back in Action

Acting head coach Shaun Harris has made just two crucial changes to the lineup that secured a 1-0 victory over the Houston Dash. These aren't just any changes; they're game-changers that signal Gotham FC's intent.

**Bruninha**, the two-time NWSL champion, is making her first start of the season. Slotted into the right side of the backline for her 85th club appearance, the 24-year-old Brazilian brings vital experience and defensive prowess. She'll be working in tandem with the formidable Guro Reiten on the left, who has been a force with four assists since April 29 and earned NWSL Best XI of the Month honors for July. Alongside center backs Jess Carter and captain Tierna Davidson, this defensive unit looks primed to withstand any pressure from the Wave.

Perhaps the biggest buzz, however, surrounds the return of veteran goalkeeper **Ann-Katrin Berger**. After resting against Bay FC and the Houston Dash, Berger is back in goal, chasing not one, but two significant milestones. A ninth shutout of 2026 would place her second in club history for clean sheets in a single season. Even more impressively, her next win would surpass Kailen Sheridan (San Diego) for eighth in league history for a single club. Berger's presence between the posts is a psychological boost and a tactical advantage.

## Gotham FC's Star-Studded Lineup: Kerr, Lavelle & More to Watch

Gotham FC (11-3-4, 37 points) enters this match atop the NWSL standings, having amassed an impressive seven points from their recent three-match road swing through Portland, San Jose, and Houston. A victory here would extend their lead over the second-place Washington Spirit by four points, putting significant pressure on their rivals before the weekend's full slate of matches.

The midfield boasts a familiar and dominant trio: **Rose Lavelle**, **Jaelin Howell**, and **Savannah McCaskill**. This combination, starting together for the fourth consecutive match and 13th time this season, has been instrumental in Gotham's success. Both Lavelle (celebrating her 50th Gotham FC start) and Howell earned spots alongside Reiten in the league’s Best XI for July, with Howell particularly shining with two assists and double-digit tackles won, clearances, and interceptions.

Leading the attacking charge is star striker **Sam Kerr**, flanked by **Jaedyn Shaw** on the left and rookie sensation **Jordynn Dudley** on the right. This dynamic attacking trio has accounted for eight of Gotham FC’s goals this season. Kerr, in particular, is in red-hot form, having scored the game-winner against the Dash and tying a club record with her 30th regular-season goal for Gotham FC. Expect her to be a constant threat.

## Gotham FC Injury Report: Who's Sidelined for the San Diego Wave Match?

While the starting lineup is strong, Gotham FC does contend with some absences. Midfielder Denise O’Sullivan is out with a knee injury, per the player availability report. Defenders Kayla Duran and Emily Sonnett are both on the season-ending injury list, also with knee issues.

The bench offers depth, featuring goalkeeper Shelby Hogan; defender Mandy Freeman; midfielders Taryn Torres, Sofia Cook, Sarah Schupansky, Talia Sommer, and newest signing Meredith Speck; and forwards Andrea Kitahata and Midge Purce.

This Friday's match is more than just points; it's about momentum, records, and a statement from the NWSL's current leaders. Can Gotham FC deliver? You won't want to miss it.
