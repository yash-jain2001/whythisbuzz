## Key Referee Picked for Santos vs Vitória: Why This Appointment Matters

The Brasileirão's 18th round is heating up, and all eyes are on the upcoming clash between Santos and Vitória this Saturday, May 30th, at 8 PM. But beyond the battle on the pitch, a significant decision has been made off it: the Brazilian Football Confederation (CBF) has tapped **Rafael Rodrigo Klein**, a FIFA-badged referee from Rio Grande do Sul, to officiate the crucial encounter. Why is this specific appointment garnering attention? Let's break down the buzz.

This isn't Klein's first rodeo with Vitória in the 2026 season. In fact, this will be his **third match** overseeing the Bahia club this year alone. His previous outings with Vitória have been notable:

*   **A hard-fought 1-1 draw** in the intense Ba-Vi derby during the 5th round.
*   **A dominant 4-1 victory** for Vitória against Coritiba in the 14th round.

This repeated assignment means Klein is no stranger to Vitória's style of play or the pressure surrounding their matches. A match of this magnitude demands a top-tier officiating team, and the CBF has assembled a strong lineup to support Klein. Here's who will be ensuring fair play:

*   **Referee:** Rafael Rodrigo Klein (RS-FIFA)
*   **Assistant 1:** Maira Mastella Moreira (RS-FIFA)
*   **Assistant 2:** Maurício Coelho Silva Penna (RS)
