## Rhys Carre's Rugby Revival: From Overlooked to Unstoppable for Wales

Remember Rhys Carre? The Saracens prop who, not long ago, seemed to be on the fringes of the Welsh international rugby scene? Well, he's back, and he's not just back – he's making headlines, scoring tries, and becoming an unlikely social media sensation. His secret weapon? A coach who simply lets him "do what he does best."

For years, Carre faced public criticism from former coaches Warren Gatland and Wayne Pivac, who cited missed individual performance targets as reasons for his exclusion. It was a tough period, leaving many to wonder if the powerful prop would ever truly fulfill his international potential. But enter Steve Tandy, Wales' current head coach, and suddenly, the narrative has flipped.

### The Steve Tandy Effect: How a Coach Unlocked Rhys Carre's True Potential

Carre himself is quick to credit Tandy, acknowledging that the new boss has not only restored his belief but also empowered him on the field. This isn't just about tactical changes; it's about a fundamental shift in coaching philosophy that allows players like Carre to play instinctively, embracing their unique strengths without being shackled by rigid expectations. Tandy's approach seems to have provided the psychological boost Carre desperately needed, translating directly into his electrifying performances.

The results speak for themselves. Carre's 30-meter try against Ireland not only sent the crowd wild but also broke the internet, solidifying his status as a fan favourite and proving that props, traditionally known for their power in the scrum, can also possess surprising pace and attacking flair.

### Why Rhys Carre's Try-Scoring Streak is Making History for Wales

But the Ireland try wasn't a fluke. Carre has cemented his comeback by achieving a truly remarkable feat: he's become only the sixth prop in rugby history to score in three successive Test matches. This incredible scoring streak is practically unheard of for a front-row forward and highlights his newfound confidence and attacking prowess. To put it in perspective, he's currently Wales' leading points scorer in the 2026 championship – a stat usually reserved for fly-halves or prolific wingers, not a bruising prop.

This dramatic turnaround isn't just a personal triumph for Carre; it's a testament to the transformative power of belief and effective coaching. Tandy's willingness to trust his players and give them the freedom to express themselves is clearly paying dividends, not just for Carre, but for the entire Welsh squad. As Carre continues to defy expectations and rack up points, the buzz around his redemption story is only growing louder, proving that sometimes, all a player needs is a coach who truly sees their potential.
