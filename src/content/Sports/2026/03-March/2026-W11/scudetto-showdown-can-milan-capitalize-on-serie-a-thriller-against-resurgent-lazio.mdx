The Serie A title race just got a whole lot spicier. AC Milan travels to the Stadio Olimpico tonight, facing Lazio in a pivotal clash that could ignite their Scudetto ambitions. With rivals Inter dropping points, the Rossoneri have a golden, five-point opportunity to close the gap at the top. Kick-off is set for 19:45 GMT (20:45 CET), and all eyes will be on how Milan handles the pressure against a Lazio side finding new momentum.

### **AC Milan's Scudetto Hopes: Capitalizing on Inter's Stumble**

Last week's Derby della Madonnina victory blew the title race wide open, and Inter's subsequent 1-1 draw against Atalanta has handed Milan an even bigger lifeline. This isn't just another fixture; it's a chance to send a powerful message and tighten the grip on their pursuit of glory.

Leading the charge for the Rossoneri will be the electrifying duo of **Rafael Leao** and **Christian Pulisic**. Their pace, creativity, and goal-scoring threat are crucial for unlocking Lazio's defense. However, Milan isn't without its own challenges. They'll be without the suspended Adrien Rabiot, while Matteo Gabbia, Ruben Loftus-Cheek, and Santiago Gimenez are sidelined with injuries, forcing tactical adjustments.

Expected to line up for Milan are: Maignan; Tomori, De Winter, Pavlovic; Saelemaekers, Fofana, Jashari, Estupinan; Leao, Pulisic. The depth of their squad will be truly tested.

### **Lazio's Resurgence: Ultras Return and Form Improves**

Across the pitch, Maurizio Sarri's Lazio is riding a wave of renewed confidence. The Biancocelesti ended a difficult spell by securing a crucial 2-1 victory against Sassuolo last week. More significantly, the club's ultras have suspended their boycott, meaning the Stadio Olimpico will be rocking with passionate chants, flags, and fireworks. This return of fervent home support could provide a vital psychological boost, turning the stadium into a fortress once more.

However, Sarri also faces a formidable injury list. Danilo Cataldi joins Alessio Romagnoli, Ivan Provedel, Toma Basic, Nicolò Rovella, and Samuel Gigot on the treatment table, forcing strategic changes. Despite the absences, players like **Daniel Maldini** (a former Milan youth player, who scored against Sassuolo) and **Mattia Zaccagni** will be key figures in Lazio's attack. Gil Patric is also expected to feature in a new midfield role, adding intrigue to their setup.

Lazio's anticipated lineup includes: Motta; Marusic, Gila, Provstgaard, Nuno Tavares; Dele-Bashiru, Patric, Taylor; Isaksen, Maldini, Zaccagni.
