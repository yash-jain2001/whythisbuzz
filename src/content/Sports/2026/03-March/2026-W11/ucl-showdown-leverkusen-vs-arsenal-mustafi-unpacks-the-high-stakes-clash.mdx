The stage is set. The lights are ready to blaze at the BayArena. In a Champions League clash promising tactical fireworks and raw emotion, Bayer Leverkusen squares off against Premier League giants FC Arsenal in the Round of 16. On Wednesday, at 18:45 (CET), the first leg ignites what many anticipate to be one of the tournament's most intriguing encounters.

But who truly holds the edge when two of Europe's most exciting teams collide? To break down the tactical nuances and psychological warfare, we turned to a man who knows both sides of this equation: World Champion and former Arsenal defender, Shkodran Mustafi. Having played 151 times for the North London club, Mustafi's insight offers a rare, unbiased look into what it will take to win.

## Unpacking the UCL Clash: Shkodran Mustafi on Leverkusen vs. Arsenal

"Arsenal is the clear favourite," Mustafi told WhyThisBuzz, cutting straight to the chase. "However, they’re facing an incredibly *unpleasant* opponent in Leverkusen." This isn't just polite punditry; it’s a warning. Leverkusen's journey to the Champions League knockout stages wasn't a fluke; it's a testament to their inherent quality and ability to "hurt any team."

### Arsenal's Dominance: How Arteta Built a Title Contender

So, what makes Arsenal the team to beat? According to Mustafi, it's a potent cocktail of quality, depth, and tactical mastery. "The squad is simply better and broader," he explains. Mikel Arteta, Arsenal's visionary manager, has spent years meticulously crafting a team capable of reaching this elite level, and it's paying off with their current Premier League dominance.

However, even champions get wobbles. Mustafi points to recent Premier League draws against Brentford (1:1) and Wolverhampton (2:2) as "warning shots." These results underscore a critical lesson: "Arsenal can only succeed if they take every opponent seriously." Complacency, even for a moment, can be fatal.

Another formidable weapon in Arsenal's arsenal? Set pieces. Their dramatic 2-1 victory against Chelsea saw them net a winning goal from a standard for the ninth time this season – a new Premier League record. "It’s an enormous quality," Mustafi emphasizes. "Teams should avoid giving away corners or fouls near the penalty area against Arsenal. They've earned that respect."

### Leverkusen's Upset Potential: Exploiting Arsenal's Tactical Vulnerabilities

While Arsenal's strengths are undeniable, Leverkusen isn't without its own sharp edges. Mustafi highlights the path to victory for the German side:

*   **Courage in 1-on-1s:** Leverkusen must be fearless in individual duels, not shying away from Arsenal's relentless pressure.
*   **Ball Security:** Maintaining possession under pressure will be crucial to disrupt Arsenal's rhythm.
*   **Attacking Deep:** "Leverkusen needs to attack over the depth," Mustafi advises. This strategy can create the very "spaces where Arsenal is vulnerable."

And while Arsenal has a set-piece ace, Leverkusen boasts its own wizard: Alejandro Grimaldo. "Arteta will certainly be aware of Grimaldo’s free-kick qualities," Mustafi adds, hinting at a potential tit-for-tat from dead-ball situations.

### Kai Havertz's Evolving Role at Arsenal: A Tactical Deep Dive

A key figure in this high-stakes encounter will be former Bayer star Kai Havertz. Having joined Arsenal in 2023, the 26-year-old German international has become a vital cog in Arteta's system. Mustafi reveals the secret behind Havertz's success: "Mikel Arteta holds him in high regard because Kai is very flexible in his game."

Arteta loves players who can seamlessly shift positions without needing a substitution, and Havertz fits that mold perfectly. Fast, strong in the air, and having significantly bulked up physically during a recent injury layoff, Havertz has adapted brilliantly to the rigors of English football. "He is highly valued within the club," Mustafi confirms.

However, if there's one player Mustafi sees as Arsenal's current linchpin, it's **Declan Rice**. "It's hard to pick one player from this squad, but Declan Rice is currently the most important player," Mustafi asserts. Why? Because he's the critical "connector of defence and offense," dictating tempo and providing both defensive steel and attacking thrust.

### Leverkusen's Resilience: A History of Punching Above Their Weight

Lest anyone forget Leverkusen's mettle, Mustafi recalls a telling past performance: "Leverkusen managed to beat Manchester City 2-0 in the league phase with a rump-elf [skeleton crew]." This impressive victory, achieved despite significant absences, "left an impression in England and will surely be a talking point for Arsenal leading up to the match."

Mustafi, who also co-commentated Leverkusen's 3-1 victory in Frankfurt in January 2026, notes the club's remarkable resilience. "One shouldn't forget how many important players left last summer; a whole axis broke away." Yet, Leverkusen has found its footing. "They have stabilised over the course of the season and appear relatively stable to me. The team's potential is clearly evident – but they are still a long way from the level of their double-winning season."

This isn't just a football match; it's a test of tactical ingenuity, player adaptability, and sheer mental fortitude. Can Leverkusen defy the odds, or will Arsenal's ambition prove too strong? This is one "Buzz" you won't want to miss.
