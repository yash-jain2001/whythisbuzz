## Las Vegas Raiders Bolster Roster: The Strategic Signing of Linebacker Segun Olubi

HENDERSON, Nev. – The Las Vegas Raiders have made a calculated move in free agency, officially announcing the signing of unrestricted free agent linebacker Segun Olubi. While not a headline-grabbing superstar, Olubi's arrival signals a clear intent from the Silver and Black to fortify a critical, often underestimated, aspect of NFL football: special teams.

This isn't just another roster fill; it's a strategic acquisition aimed at bringing consistent, high-impact play to a phase of the game that can swing momentum and even decide outcomes. For a team striving for a fresh start and aiming to climb out of recent struggles, every single edge counts.

### Why Segun Olubi's Elite Special Teams Play Defines His Value to the Raiders

Olubi joins the Raiders after a solid four-season tenure with the Indianapolis Colts (2022-25), where he became synonymous with special teams excellence. Over 51 games, his defensive stats included 20 tackles (14 solo), one pass defensed, one interception, two forced fumbles, and two fumble recoveries. However, it's his special teams output that truly distinguishes him.

During his time with the Colts, Olubi amassed a remarkable **30 special teams tackles**, leading the team in that crucial category over that span. This isn't a fluke; it's a consistent display of effort, athleticism, and football IQ that directly impacts field position and prevents big plays from opponents.

### Unpacking Olubi's 2025 Season: Key Plays and League-Wide Impact

His 2025 campaign stands out as a testament to his value. Olubi played in all 17 games, recording **11 special teams tackles**, forcing one fumble, and blocking an invaluable punt. To put this into perspective, he was one of only 13 players across the entire NFL last season to hit double-digit special teams tackles *and* record a forced fumble. This statistic alone highlights his rare ability to not just make tackles but also create turnovers and momentum-shifting plays in the game's hidden moments.

For a team like the Raiders, who are looking to maximize every possession and minimize opponent opportunities, having a player of Olubi's caliber on special teams can be the difference between a long punt return for the opposition and a crucial stop deep in their territory.

### Collegiate Roots: How Olubi Developed His Playmaking Instincts

Olubi's journey to the NFL wasn't a straight line, but his collegiate career consistently demonstrated his playmaking potential. He played at San Diego State from 2020-21, where he appeared in 21 games. During this time, he racked up 68 tackles (34 solo), four tackles for loss, 2.0 sacks, two passes defensed, a critical interception returned for a touchdown, and two forced fumbles. Before SDSU, he also spent time at Harding University (2019), Saddlebrook College (2018), and College of Idaho (2017), showcasing a resilient path through various programs. This diverse background suggests a player who understands hard work and seizing opportunities.

### The Bigger Picture: How Segun Olubi Fits the Raiders' Fresh Start Philosophy

The Raiders are undeniably in a rebuilding phase, aiming to erase the memory of a dismal 3-14 season. With a new regime and a focus on fostering a winning culture, every player brought in must contribute meaningfully. Segun Olubi embodies this philosophy. He might not be the face of the franchise, but his consistent excellence in special teams directly addresses a fundamental need for any competitive NFL team.

His signing underscores a commitment to improving in all three phases of the game – offense, defense, and special teams. A strong special teams unit can alleviate pressure on the offense, protect a struggling defense, and consistently provide favorable field position. In the tight margins of the NFL, these "small" wins can accumulate into significant advantages. The addition of Olubi is a clear signal that the Raiders are leaving no stone unturned in their quest to become a more disciplined, impactful, and ultimately, a winning football team.
