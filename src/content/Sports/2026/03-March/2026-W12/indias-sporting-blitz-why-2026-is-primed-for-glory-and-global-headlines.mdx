## Squash's Olympic Dream: Anahat Singh on the LA 28 Games Impact

The inclusion of squash in the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics has sent ripples of excitement through the sport globally, and India is no exception. This quote isn't just about personal ambition; it signifies a massive shift. Olympic recognition brings unprecedented funding, attention, and a clear aspirational goal for young athletes, potentially transforming squash's profile in India. It means more investment, better facilities, and a stronger pipeline of talent aiming for that ultimate Olympic glory.
