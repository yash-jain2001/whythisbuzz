Immediately following the main showcase, prepare for a dedicated deep dive into one of Xbox's most iconic franchises: **Gears of War**. The **E-Day Direct** will shine an exclusive spotlight on *Gears of War: E-Day*, a pivotal prequel that plunges players back to the very genesis of the saga.

Developed by the talented team at The Coalition, *Gears of War: E-Day* isn't just another installment; it's an origin story. It promises to unveil the harrowing events of Emergence Day, the cataclysmic moment when the Locust Horde first erupted from beneath Sera's surface. What makes this significant? It offers a fresh perspective on beloved characters and lore, potentially re-energizing the franchise for a new generation while satisfying long-time fans. Expect thrilling new gameplay footage and crucial behind-the-scenes insights into its development, all pointing towards a release later this year. This is a big bet for Xbox, aiming to reclaim a piece of the shooter pie with a narrative heavy-hitter.
