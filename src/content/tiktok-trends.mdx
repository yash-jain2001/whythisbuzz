Virality used to be luck. Now it's a science.

## The Psychology of Mimicry

TikTok relies on "Participatory Media." You don't just watch; you join in. Trends that are easy to replicate (dances, lip-syncs) fly faster than those that require unique talent.

## Audio is Key

Using a trending sound is the fastest way to hack the algorithm. Audio provides the connective tissue between disparate videos.
