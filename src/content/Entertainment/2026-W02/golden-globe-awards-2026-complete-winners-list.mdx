The **83rd Golden Globe Awards**, held on **January 11, 2026**, celebrated the year’s most talked-about achievements in film and television. The ceremony aired live on **CBS and Paramount+** from Beverly Hills, with **Nikki Glaser** returning as host.

From emotional acceptance speeches to dominant wins by a handful of titles, the night reflected shifting tastes in Hollywood, with strong performances from both prestige cinema and mainstream entertainment.

Below is the **complete and verified list of Golden Globe 2026 winners**, organized by category.

---

## Golden Globe Winners 2026: Movies

### Best Motion Picture – Drama
**WINNER:** *Hamnet*

### Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy
**WINNER:** *One Battle After Another*

### Best Actor – Drama
**WINNER:** **Wagner Moura**, *The Secret Agent*

### Best Actress – Drama
**WINNER:** **Jessie Buckley**, *Hamnet*

### Best Actor – Musical or Comedy
**WINNER:** **Timothée Chalamet**, *Marty Supreme*

### Best Actress – Musical or Comedy
**WINNER:** **Rose Byrne**, *If I Had Legs I'd Kick You*

### Best Supporting Actor – Motion Picture
**WINNER:** **Stellan Skarsgård**, *Sentimental Value*

### Best Supporting Actress – Motion Picture
**WINNER:** **Teyana Taylor**, *One Battle After Another*

### Best Director
**WINNER:** **Paul Thomas Anderson**, *One Battle After Another*

### Best Screenplay
**WINNER:** **Paul Thomas Anderson**, *One Battle After Another*

### Best Original Score
**WINNER:** **Ludwig Göransson**, *Sinners*

### Best Original Song
**WINNER:** **“Golden”**, *KPop Demon Hunters*

### Best Animated Film
**WINNER:** *KPop Demon Hunters*

### Best Non-English Language Film
**WINNER:** *The Secret Agent*

### Cinematic and Box Office Achievement
**WINNER:** *Sinners*
