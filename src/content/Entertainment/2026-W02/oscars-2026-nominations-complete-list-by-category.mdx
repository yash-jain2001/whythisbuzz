The Oscar nominations for 2026 have officially been announced, setting the stage for the **98th Academy Awards**, scheduled to take place on **March 15, 2026**. As always, the nominations offer an early look at how the Academy is recognizing the most talked-about films, performances, and creative achievements of the year.

This year’s ceremony will once again be hosted by **Conan O’Brien**, marking his second consecutive appearance as host. From major studio releases to critically acclaimed international films, the nominations reflect a wide range of cinematic styles and storytelling voices.

Below is the **complete list of Oscar nominees for 2026**, organized category-wise.
