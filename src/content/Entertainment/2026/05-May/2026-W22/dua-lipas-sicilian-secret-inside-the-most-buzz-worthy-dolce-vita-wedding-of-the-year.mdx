## Why Did Dua Lipa Choose Sicily for Her Secret Wedding?

The romance between the 30-year-old "Houdini" singer and the 36-year-old "Masters of the Air" star has been a whirlwind, culminating in a reported three-day celebration from **June 5th to 7th** in the stunning northwestern region of Sicily, centered around Palermo.

But why Sicily? It seems the island holds a special place in their hearts. Last summer, the couple was spotted enjoying the Sicilian capital, with Lipa sharing snapshots on Instagram captioned, "Palermo in my ❤️❤️❤️❤️". From devouring cannolis on Via Maqueda to playing cards on historic cobblestones, strolling through winding alleys, and sipping wine at local trattorias, their previous visits clearly cemented Sicily as *the* spot for their big day. It’s more than just a destination; it's a personal connection that adds an intimate layer to the grand affair.

## Unveiling the Lavish Venues for Dua Lipa's Wedding Extravaganza

The leaked plans suggest an itinerary steeped in history and grandeur, transforming iconic Sicilian locations into scenes for their "Dolce Vita" dream.

### Where Will Dua Lipa and Callum Turner Say "I Do"?

The centerpiece of the festivities is rumored to be the **Villa Valguarnera**, an exquisite 18th-century historical estate nestled in Bagheria, just 20 kilometers east of Palermo. This isn't the villa's first rodeo with celebrity events; its pomp and splendor have previously hosted high-profile weddings, film shoots, and fashion editorials. Expect nothing less than cinematic elegance for Lipa and Turner's vows.

Beyond the villa, the celebrations are expected to spill into Palermo itself, with the **Galleria d'Arte Moderna** and the nearby **Piazza Croce dei Vespri** mentioned as additional backdrops. For accommodation, the bridal party and most guests are reportedly taking over the entire **Villa Igiea**, a five-star luxury hotel in Palermo, which is completely booked from June 5th to 7th. It's safe to say this will be an unforgettable stay for everyone involved.

## Who's on the Guest List for Dua Lipa's Star-Studded Nuptials?

Expect to see music legends like **Elton John**, alongside rising stars such as **Olivia Dean** and **Charli XCX**. But perhaps the most exciting leaked name is fashion icon **Donatella Versace**. And *this* is where things get truly buzzy: Versace is reportedly not just attending, but has also designed and created Lipa's wedding dress, in collaboration with a Palermo atelier. Talk about a fashion moment that will break the internet!

## The Mastermind Behind the "Dolce Vita" Dream: Who is Dua Lipa's Wedding Planner?

Pulling off a three-day, multi-venue, super-secret celebrity wedding of this scale requires a serious pro, and **Alessandra Grillo** is reportedly the woman for the job. Grillo is no stranger to high-stakes celebrity nuptials; she was also the architect behind the infamous mega-wedding of Italian super-influencer Chiara Ferragni and rapper Fedez.

**Why this matters for the buzz:** While Grillo clearly delivers on spectacle, the Ferragni-Fedez union ultimately ended in separation. Even the most perfectly planned wedding can't guarantee a fairytale ending – a reminder that behind the "Dolce Vita" facade, real life, and real pressures, continue.
