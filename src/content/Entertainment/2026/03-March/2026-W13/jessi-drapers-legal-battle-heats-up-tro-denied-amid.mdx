## Jessi Draper's TRO Request Against Jordan Ngatikaura Denied: What It Means for Their Divorce

The legal fireworks just got a whole lot more explosive for "Secret Lives of Mormon Wives" star Jessi Draper and her estranged husband, Jordan Ngatikaura. In a move that sends ripples through the reality TV world, Ngatikaura’s request for a temporary restraining order (TRO) against Draper has been **denied**, Page Six exclusively confirms. This isn't just a minor legal setback; it's a significant development in a divorce saga already overflowing with public accusations and high-stakes drama.

Ngatikaura, known as a "DadTok" personality, sought the TRO last Friday amidst their ongoing divorce proceedings. Draper, 33, wasted no time, filing an opposition later that same day. While reps for both parties remain tight-lipped, the denial suggests the court found insufficient grounds for the immediate protection Ngatikaura sought, leaving their tumultuous separation to play out in the public eye with even more intensity.

### The Unraveling of Jessi Draper and Jordan Ngatikaura's Marriage

Their union, once celebrated five years ago, has reportedly been on thin ice for a considerable time. The couple, who share two young children – son Jagger, 5, and daughter Jovi, 3 – had allegedly separated multiple times before Ngatikaura officially filed for divorce last Thursday.

In a recent statement, Ngatikaura admitted the split was "not an easy decision," emphasizing his commitment to his children. "While our paths are now moving in different directions, my priority remains my children and ensuring they feel loved, supported, and protected through this transition," he stated, adding a vow to handle the next chapter "with kindness and respect." Noble words, but the recent TRO attempt paints a picture of a far more contentious reality behind the scenes.

### Past Accusations: Jessi Draper's Claims of Emotional Abuse and Emotional Affairs

The current legal tussle doesn't exist in a vacuum. Draper herself has openly discussed the deep-seated issues within their marriage. In a revealing 2025 interview with People, the hairdresser publicly accused Ngatikaura of **emotional abuse**. She vividly described how "little behaviors over time" can snowball, leaving one feeling "too deep in it and you can’t do much about it." This accusation provides critical context, highlighting a potentially toxic dynamic that predates the divorce filing.

During the same interview, Draper also confessed to an **emotional affair** with "Vanderpump Villa" star Marciano Brunette. While not physical, emotional infidelity can be just as devastating, further illustrating the profound cracks in her relationship with Ngatikaura. These past revelations underscore the complexities and potential motivations behind the recent legal maneuvers. Was the TRO an attempt to gain leverage, or a genuine cry for protection in a relationship marked by years of alleged turmoil?
