The buzz is palpable, and for good reason. Prime Video’s hit comedy gauntlet, *Last One Laughing*, has returned for a second season, bringing with it a fresh roster of comedic heavyweights. Among the familiar faces like David Mitchell and Romesh Ranganathan, one name is rapidly rising to the forefront: **Amy Gledhill**. But while her straight-faced antics on screen are earning laughs, Gledhill’s journey off-screen reveals a depth and resilience that truly makes her a standout figure in modern comedy.

## What's the Buzz Around Amy Gledhill on Last One Laughing Season 2?

Hosted by the inimitable Jimmy Carr, *Last One Laughing* pits ten top comedians against each other in a bizarre, high-stakes endurance test: make your rivals laugh, but *do not* crack a smile yourself. With a lineup that includes Diane Morgan, Maisie Adam, and Sam Campbell, the pressure is immense. The first three episodes of season two are already streaming, and Gledhill is proving herself a formidable player, navigating the comedic minefield with her signature wit. It's a show designed to push comedians to their limits, and watching Gledhill hold her own is a testament to her unique comedic power.

### Understanding Amy Gledhill: From Hull to Edinburgh Award Winner

So, who is Amy Gledhill, and why is she perfectly suited for this unique challenge? A proud Yorkshire export, Gledhill honed her observational comedy skills moving between Hull, Leeds, Manchester, and eventually, London. Her style is relatable, sharp, and often delivered with a disarming charm that belies its underlying intelligence.

Her talent isn't just about making people laugh on a game show; it's critically acclaimed. In 2024, Gledhill clinched the prestigious **Edinburgh Comedy Award** for her critically lauded show, "Make Me Look Fit On The Poster." This isn't just an award; it's a significant marker of comedic excellence, signalling her as one of the most exciting voices in British stand-up. Beyond her solo work, she's one half of the beloved absurdist duo **The Delightful Sausage** with Chris Cantrill, further showcasing her versatility and collaborative spirit.

## Amy Gledhill's Diverse On-Screen Career: From Netflix to Taskmaster

While *Last One Laughing* might be her current high-profile gig, Gledhill is no stranger to the screen. Her acting resume spans both comedic and dramatic roles, highlighting her range beyond the stand-up stage:

*   **Netflix's Sex Education:** Audiences might recall her memorable turn as the beautician who handled Joanna’s (Lisa McGrillis) waxing in the fourth series, bringing her dry wit to a much-loved show.
*   **Netflix's Run Away:** She took on a more serious role as **DC Ruby Todd** in the thriller, proving her capability in dramatic storytelling.
*   **Other Notable Appearances:** Gledhill has also graced screens in *Alma's Not Normal*, *Somewhere Boy*, and *Life*.
*   **Channel 4's Toads:** Two years ago, she not only starred in but also **penned** this acclaimed short film, demonstrating her writing prowess.
*   **Taskmaster 2026 Cast:** Looking ahead, fans can anticipate her joining the celebrity chaos of *Taskmaster*, a show that perfectly aligns with her quick thinking and playful nature.

This extensive portfolio shows Gledhill isn't just a comedian; she's a versatile performer and writer who understands how to connect with an audience across various mediums.

## The Heart Behind the Humor: Amy Gledhill's Recent Personal Loss

Just days before *Last One Laughing* premiered, Gledhill shared a deeply personal and heartbreaking update with her followers: the passing of her beloved father on March 13, 2026. Her Instagram tribute offered a poignant glimpse into the man who shaped her, describing him as "embarrassingly supportive," giving her a standing ovation at her fourth ever gig.

"He was heartbreakingly sentimental, the most generous guy in the pub, a proper hard worker, a great singer, incredible on the dance floor and he was cracking jokes and making us laugh right until the end," she wrote. This raw, honest outpouring not only humanizes a comedian known for her sharp observations but also reinforces the resilience often found in performers who use humor to navigate life's toughest moments. It's a powerful reminder that behind every laugh, there's a person with a rich, complex story.

## Amy Gledhill Champions Mental Health: Using Humor for Good

Beyond the stage and screen, Amy Gledhill is using her platform for an incredibly important cause. In 2026, she proudly took on the role of patron for **Hull and East Yorkshire Mind**, a mental health charity. Her mission? To infuse "approachable humour" into discussions surrounding mental well-being.

As Gledhill eloquently put it, "I've always believed that supporting your mental health is as important as supporting Hull City – and in both cases, sometimes you really need someone to lean on." This commitment showcases her desire to leverage her comedic gift for social impact, breaking down stigmas and making it easier for people to talk about their struggles. It’s a testament to her belief that laughter can not only entertain but also heal and connect.

From her witty battle on *Last One Laughing* to her heartfelt personal disclosures and dedicated advocacy, Amy Gledhill is more than just a comedian; she's a compelling voice of her generation. She’s navigating the complexities of life, loss, and laughter with an authenticity that resonates deeply, making her an artist truly worth watching.
