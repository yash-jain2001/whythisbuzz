The buzz is real, the wait is over, and Hilary Duff has officially landed back on stage in a big way. The beloved singer and actress has just unleashed "The Lucky Me Tour," and if opening night is anything to go by, fans are in for an unforgettable ride. WhyThisBuzz is here for the deep dive into what makes this tour a major moment for Duff and the pop music landscape.

## The Grand Unveiling: What's On Hilary Duff's Lucky Me Tour Setlist?

Hilary Duff launched her highly anticipated tour in spectacular fashion at the iTHINK Financial Amphitheatre in West Palm Beach, Florida, on Sunday night (June 21). This isn't just a casual return; it's a full-throttle expansion of her earlier "Luck… or Something" album mini-tour, upgrading from intimate venues to arenas and amphitheaters worldwide.

The initial shows for her new album were just a taste, and now the main course is being served. Fans have been eagerly awaiting an expanded experience, and Duff has delivered, adding more tracks to truly fill the larger stages and satisfy a hungry audience.

### Rare Gems & New Tracks: The Live Debuts Fans Are Raving About

The opening night setlist wasn't just a collection of hits; it was a carefully curated journey through Duff's discography, blending the new with the nostalgic. Among the absolute highlights were the live debuts of three tracks: **"Growing Up," "Holiday Party," and "Adult Size Medium."** These fresh additions give fans a first listen to how new material translates to the live stage, injecting modern energy into her performances.

But perhaps even more electrifying for long-time fans were the returns of **"Play With Fire" and "Anywhere but Here"** to the setlist. These songs haven't been performed live in nearly two decades! This isn't just about playing old songs; it's a powerful nod to her enduring legacy and a reward for the dedicated fans who've been with her since the early days. It’s a moment of collective nostalgia that deepens the connection between artist and audience.

## Hilary Duff's Global Domination: Where to Catch The Lucky Me Tour in 2026 & 2027

"The Lucky Me Tour" is far from a localized event. Hilary Duff is setting out to conquer stages across the globe. Her North American leg will run through mid-August, giving fans across the continent ample opportunity to witness her return.

But the journey doesn't stop there. Throughout September and October, Duff will grace stages in Europe, New Zealand, and Australia, ensuring her international fanbase gets their moment. The tour extends into early 2027, with planned stops in Canada and Mexico. This extensive global itinerary underscores the massive demand for Duff's music and her enduring appeal as a pop culture figure worldwide.

### The Setlist Disclaimer: What Fans Need to Know About Tour Changes

While the opening night setlist provides an exciting glimpse into what's to come, a critical note for concert-goers: **this setlist is representative of the first show and might not be completely accurate for every performance.** Artists often tweak their setlists throughout a tour based on audience reaction, vocal performance, or simply to keep things fresh. So, while you can expect many of these incredible songs, prepare for potential surprises!

"The Lucky Me Tour" isn't just a series of concerts; it's a testament to her enduring power, relevance, and the magnetic pull she still holds over pop culture.
