The air backstage at "Immer wieder sonntags" was electric on July 19th, but for one legendary figure, it was also thick with emotion. **Schlager king Jürgen Drews (81)** stood hand-in-hand with his wife Ramona (52), eyes glued to a monitor, as their daughter **Joelina Drews (30)** commanded the stage. What unfolded wasn't just another performance; it was a deeply personal moment, a symbolic passing of the torch in front of millions.

## Joelina Drews' "Durch und Durch" Performance on ARD's Immer wieder sonntags

Joelina, radiant and confident, delivered her latest single, **"Durch und Durch."** The track isn't just new; it's a fresh take on her father's iconic 1995 hit, a poignant bridge connecting generations of German music. As the melody filled the studio in Rust, Jürgen Drews, looking sharp in an open colorful shirt, white trousers, and a casual blazer, couldn't hold back his tears of pride.

This wasn't just a proud dad moment; it was validation for a father who's seen it all and now gets to witness his legacy flourish through his daughter. "I'm really enjoying this," Drews told BILD backstage. "I can watch completely relaxed, and I've met up with some colleagues again. I've practically **handed the baton over to Joelina**." He wasn't just speaking about a song; he was talking about a career, a genre, and a spotlight he once dominated. "I'm very proud of her, and I think she's doing great. A super performance! Ramona and I are absolutely thrilled. Joelina will go her own way, I'm absolutely sure of that."

### Jürgen Drews' Emotional Reunions and Health Update Backstage

The Sunday show wasn't just about Joelina's stellar act. It was also a chance for Jürgen to reconnect with old friends. He shared a joyful reunion with **"Immer wieder sonntags" host Stefan Mross (50)** – a meeting made more significant as it's Mross's final season at the helm. Actor **Mark Keller (61)** also joined the celebration, with Drews quipping about Keller's "beautiful whistling." These moments offered a glimpse into the camaraderie that defines the Schlager world, underscoring the deep connections Drews has fostered over decades.

Joelina herself was deeply moved by her parents' presence. "I'm incredibly happy that my dad is here today and that I can give something back to him for once," she shared with BILD. "Usually, I've always accompanied him; today, it's the other way around. It's a truly beautiful feeling for me to have so much support here. And I'm happy that Dad is enjoying the atmosphere and reconnecting with colleagues." It was a role reversal that highlighted the shift in the Drews family spotlight, a graceful transition from one generation to the next.

### Navigating Life with Polyneuropathy: Jürgen Drews' Journey

Just a week prior, at "Klassik am Odeonsplatz" in Munich, Jürgen Drews made headlines for a different reason: he was seen using a rollator. The Schlager icon has been candid about his battle with **Polyneuropathy**, a progressive neurological condition that impairs nerve function and makes walking challenging. While his rollator was reportedly backstage at "Immer wieder sonntags," Drews' ability to be present, engaged, and visibly enjoying his daughter's success speaks volumes about his resilience and determination. It's a powerful reminder that even as physical challenges mount, the spirit of family and the joy of music can overcome.

This performance wasn't just a hit song; it was a generational crescendo, a heartfelt exchange between father and daughter, and a testament to enduring talent. Joelina Drews is not just following in her father's footsteps; she's stepping confidently into her own spotlight, with the full emotional backing of a true Schlager legend.
