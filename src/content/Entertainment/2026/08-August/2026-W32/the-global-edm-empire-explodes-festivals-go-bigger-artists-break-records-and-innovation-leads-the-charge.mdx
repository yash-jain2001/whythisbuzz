## EDC Las Vegas's Monumental Expansion: What Two Weekends Means for Fans

In a landmark decision that ripples across the entire festival landscape, **EDC Las Vegas** is set to expand to **two consecutive weekends in 2027**, marking a monumental shift in its 30-year history. Insomniac founder Pasquale Rotella confirmed the move, which will see the iconic festival run from May 14–16 (DUSK) and May 21–23 (DAWN) at the Las Vegas Motor Speedway.

This isn't just about doubling the fun; it's a strategic enhancement of the fan experience. With reduced capacity per weekend, attendees can anticipate improved crowd flow and an even more immersive journey Under the Electric Sky. Both weekends will feature the *exact same lineup*, ensuring no one misses out, while an extended "Dusk Till Dawn Experience" will blanket Las Vegas with additional programming. Accessible ticket pricing, starting at $399 USD for a single weekend and $599 USD for a combined pass, aims to bring more Headliners into the fold. This expansion solidifies EDC Las Vegas's position as a global leader, aligning it with other multi-weekend titans like Tomorrowland and Coachella.

### Celebrating 30 Years of EDC: The 2026 "kineticJOURNEY"

Before the 2027 split, **EDC Las Vegas 2026** is gearing up for a spectacular 30th-anniversary celebration, May 15–17. With a massive lineup of over 200 artists spread across its signature multi-stage landscape, the "kineticJOURNEY" theme promises a tribute to the festival's evolution from underground rave to global phenomenon.

Headliners like **Kaskade, John Summit, Martin Garrix, and FISHER** will ignite the kineticFIELD, while cosmicMEADOW offers a genre-spanning program from **Underworld** to **The Prodigy**. Techno enthusiasts will flock to neonGARDEN for **Peggy Gou** and **Joseph Capriati**, and bassPOD will rumble with **ATLiens** and **Liquid Stranger**. The sheer breadth of talent, covering house, trance, hard dance, and underground sounds, ensures every corner of electronic music culture is championed. Unsurprisingly, tickets for this milestone event are already completely sold out, emphasizing the enduring demand for the EDC experience.

## Wildlands Festival: Australia's NYE Dancefloor Revolution

Down Under, the **Wildlands** festival is reclaiming its New Year's Eve glory in Brisbane and Perth (December 31, 2026, and January 2, 2027, respectively) with one of Australia's strongest electronic music bills for the summer. This year, Wildlands is laser-focused on the dancefloor.

Headlining the meticulously curated lineup are global dance music sensation **John Summit**, British grime pioneer **Skepta**, Amsterdam rave queen **KI/KI**, and the highly anticipated Australian debut of drum and bass supergroup **WORSHIP** (comprising **Sub Focus, Dimension, Culture Shock, and 1991**). WORSHIP's appearance marks their *only* Australian performances, making Wildlands a must-attend for D&B fans. The festival promises diverse soundscapes across its Sahara, Summit, Wilds, and Portal stages, ensuring a full spectrum of house, techno, trance, jungle, and UK garage, firmly cementing its reputation as a premier destination for electronic music lovers.

## Artist Spotlights: Record-Breaking Crowds, Innovative Albums, and Next-Gen Tours

Beyond the festival grounds, individual artists are pushing their own boundaries, captivating audiences on an unprecedented scale.

### Calvin Harris Draws Record 1.6 Million Fans in São Paulo

Superstar DJ **Calvin Harris** just cemented his legend status by performing to an estimated **1.6 million people** at the Bloco Skol pre-Carnival street celebration in São Paulo, Brazil. This mind-boggling turnout transforms the event into arguably the **largest single-artist DJ performance in history**. Harris's astonishment, shared across social media, highlights the unparalleled passion of Brazilian crowds and the sheer global reach of electronic music. This wasn't just a concert; it was a cultural moment, reaffirming Brazil's powerhouse status in the dance music world.

### Skrillex Unleashes "SOMA": A Global Club Statement

Few artists consistently redefine electronic music like **Skrillex**. With the release of his latest studio album, **"SOMA,"** Sonny Moore once again proves why he remains a leading innovator. The 13-track project, released via OWSLA and Atlantic Records, is a confident and fully realized body of work reflecting the current state of global club culture.

Spanning house, bass, techno, UK sounds, Latin rhythms, and experimental club music, "SOMA" captures Skrillex's creative freedom and collaborative spirit. Featuring a diverse roster of producers and vocalists like **Feid, Young Miko, and Cristale**, the album is a snapshot of electronic music in 2026 – expansive, intentional, and constantly evolving. "SOMA" further cements Skrillex's reputation as an artist who consistently challenges expectations, with one eye firmly on the future.

### Anyma's "ÆDEN" World Tour: Blurring Music and Visual Art

Italian electronic visionary **Anyma** (one half of Tale Of Us) is set to embark on his ambitious **"ÆDEN" global tour**, launching with the debut of his next-generation live show at Coachella this April. Known for his cinematic storytelling and technological prowess, "ÆDEN" promises to fuse science fiction futurism with ancient mythological symbolism through immersive visuals and AI-driven design.

Following headline performances at the Pyramids of Giza and a historic residency at the Las Vegas Sphere, Anyma's "ÆDEN" tour (starting May 2 in China, with stops in London, Milan, Mexico City, Sydney, and more) will continue to blur the lines between concert and visual theatre. This tour, along with a renewed Ibiza residency, underscores the growing demand for highly immersive, technologically advanced live music experiences.

The electronic music landscape in 2026-27 is not just thriving; it's a testament to continuous innovation, global connectivity, and the enduring power of the dancefloor. From massive festival expansions to artists breaking new ground, the beat goes on, louder and more vibrantly than ever.
