The algorithm is always changing, but the 2026 update feels different. 

## Focus on "Originality"

Instagram is explicitly penalizing aggregators and repost accounts. If you didn't make it, it won't fly.

## Shares > Likes

The most important metric is now "Shares." If people send your content to DMs, the algorithm boosts it. Likes are a vanity metric; shares are the growth engine.
