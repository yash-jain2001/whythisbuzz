Wednesday night's Powerball drawing, an estimated $172 million, saw millions of hopefuls checking their tickets, but the grand prize remained elusive. The numbers have been called, the dust settled, and the dream of an instant fortune rolls over once more.

For those who played on Wednesday, May 29, 2026, here are the numbers you needed:

*   **Winning White Balls:** 5, 14, 21, 31, 51
*   **Red Powerball:** 13
*   **Power Play Multiplier:** 4x

Unfortunately, no ticket matched all six numbers, meaning the colossal jackpot for Wednesday's drawing goes unclaimed. The same fate met the $1 million prize for matching five white balls without the Powerball.

### What Were the Double Play Winning Numbers?

The Powerball's companion game, Double Play, also had its drawing with a top prize of $10 million. Here are those results:

*   **Double Play Numbers:** 4, 9, 43, 62, 68
*   **Double Play Powerball:** 3

Like its big brother, the Double Play jackpot and the $500,000 second-tier prize also went unmatched.

### What's the Next Powerball Jackpot and When is the Next Drawing?

With no big winner, the Powerball jackpot now climbs back to an estimated **$172 million** for the **Saturday, May 30, 2026** drawing. The cash option for this life-changing sum is an estimated $75.6 million.

Powerball drawings take place three times a week, ensuring regular chances to win:
*   **Monday**
*   **Wednesday**

All drawings are held at approximately 10:59 p.m. ET.

### Decoding Your Powerball Ticket: Understanding Prize Tiers and How to Play

A standard Powerball ticket costs $2. For an additional $1, players can add **Power Play**, which multiplies non-jackpot winnings by up to 10x (depending on the multiplier drawn, which was 4x on Wednesday). Another $1 adds **Double Play**, offering a second chance to win up to $10 million with the same set of numbers.

You don't need to match all six numbers to win a prize. Even matching just the Powerball number will net you $4. Matching one of the five white balls *and* the Powerball also wins $4. For a full breakdown of prize tiers, visit powerball.com.

The Mega Millions lottery also ramped up the excitement, with its jackpot now soaring into blockbuster territory after no winners in the latest drawing.

## The Buzz Factor: Why Do Lottery Jackpots Keep Soaring?

The sheer scale of these rollovers fuels the public's fascination. With combined jackpots now well over half a billion dollars, the "what if" factor becomes undeniably potent. This surge in prize money often leads to increased ticket sales, which in turn drives the jackpot even higher if no one wins, creating a thrilling cycle of anticipation.

The dream of financial freedom, the ability to erase debt, travel the world, or secure one's family's future, all hinge on a $2 ticket and a handful of numbers. While the odds remain astronomical, the possibility, however slim, keeps millions invested in the lottery dream, proving that hope truly springs eternal for the chance at a life-altering win. As these jackpots continue to climb, expect the buzz, and the lines at lottery retailers, to grow even longer.
