## The FTSE 250's Unexpected Stars: Unmasking Mid-Cap Outperformers in a Lagging Market

Let's face it: the FTSE 250 hasn't exactly been setting the world alight in 2026. While its big brother, the FTSE 100, has enjoyed a somewhat steadier ride, the mid-cap index has, on the whole, lagged behind. But zoom in a little closer, and you'll spot some truly remarkable individual stories – companies that aren't just performing well, but are *exploding* onto the scene.

We're talking about two particular businesses, **Raspberry Pi** (LSE:RPI) and **CMC Markets** (LSE:CMCX), which have quietly staged breathtaking rallies since the start of the year. Their share prices have surged, defying the broader index's lukewarm performance and catching the eye of savvy analysts. But what's fueling this incredible growth, and more importantly, can it last?

## Raspberry Pi's Meteoric Rise: From Hobby Board to Industrial Computing Powerhouse

Remember the credit-card-sized computers beloved by hobbyists and educators? That's Raspberry Pi. But don't let its humble origins fool you. Since its 2024 London Stock Exchange listing, this Cambridge-based company has shed its "charity-adjacent" image, transforming into a serious industrial computing player.

The numbers speak for themselves: Raspberry Pi's stock has **surged a phenomenal 165%** since January. Their recent trading update paints a picture of explosive demand, projecting over 4 million units sold in the first half of 2026 and a significant upgrade to their full-year EBITDA guidance, now expected to be well above the market consensus of $42 million.

### Why Raspberry Pi is Surging: The Untapped Potential of AI Edge Computing Demand

So, what's behind this incredible trajectory? The driver isn't just hobbyists anymore. A massive wave of demand is coming from **Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) customers** who are integrating Raspberry Pi's technology to build out cutting-edge AI edge computing power and industrial automation systems. With global AI-related spending continuing to accelerate, this trend looks less like a temporary spike and more like a long-term, powerful tailwind. Raspberry Pi is perfectly positioned at the intersection of affordable computing and industrial AI.

### The Cloud on the Horizon: Memory Shortages and Margin Compression Risks for RPI

However, even the most impressive growth stories have their vulnerabilities. For much of its recent success, Raspberry Pi has cleverly leveraged existing memory stockpiles to keep manufacturing costs low. But those stockpiles are now running low. This is a critical development, as memory prices across the tech sector are skyrocketing.

The implications are clear: if Raspberry Pi has to start buying memory at significantly higher prices, its **gross margins could face serious compression risks** in the latter half of 2026. After such an impressive bull run, a sudden deterioration in profitability could easily trigger a painful sell-off, leaving investors to wonder if the peak has passed.

## CMC Markets: Riding the Wave of New Business Models, Not Just Trading Volumes

Shifting gears, we look at CMC Markets, an online financial trading platform distinct from Raspberry Pi. CMC allows both retail and institutional investors to engage in activities like Contracts for Difference (CfDs), spread betting, and other stockbroking services. Traditionally, their revenue is tied to transaction fees, meaning profits ebb and flow with trading activity.

Yet, CMC Markets' stock has **climbed an equally impressive 141%** over the same period, baffling some who might expect market volatility to be the sole driver. This newfound momentum isn't just about increased trading; it's stemming from a crucial strategic shift.

### Beyond Transaction Fees: How Strategic Partnerships Fueled CMC's Growth

The secret sauce for CMC Markets' recent surge lies in its **new business partnerships**. Third-party operators are now able to integrate and leverage CMC's robust platform instead of investing heavily to build their own trading infrastructure. This pivot has unlocked a rapidly expanding, low-effort, high-margin revenue stream for CMC.

The impact of this strategy is evident in their latest trading update, which revealed a massive guidance upgrade for their 2027 fiscal year (ending March). Net operating income is now expected to hit at least £550 million, a substantial leap from earlier projections of £460 million-£480 million. This demonstrates a successful diversification of their revenue base beyond just individual trading activity.

### The Volatility Trap: Market Dependence and Future Headwinds for CMCX

Like Raspberry Pi, CMC Markets isn't without its weak spots. Structurally, the company remains highly dependent on overall trading activity to generate its transaction fees. While the current market environment, characterized by increased volatility, is arguably benefiting them, this cycle won't last forever. When market conditions eventually cool or stabilize, the underlying growth driven purely by trading volumes could slow considerably, or even reverse.

This dependence on market sentiment, coupled with the potential for increased competition, represents a real risk that investors must carefully consider. While the new partnership model offers diversification, the core business remains sensitive to broader economic and financial market trends.

## The Buzz on Bold Bets: Navigating Growth and Risk in the Mid-Cap Market

Both Raspberry Pi and CMC Markets are compelling stories of mid-cap companies defying broader market trends to deliver exceptional returns in 2026. Raspberry Pi leverages the insatiable demand for AI at the edge, while CMC Markets innovates with a platform-as-a-service model for financial trading.

However, their journeys are not without potential pitfalls. Raspberry Pi faces the looming threat of memory price surges, which could erode its impressive margins. CMC Markets, despite its strategic shifts, still carries the inherent risk of market-dependent revenue streams.

For investors, these companies exemplify the exciting, yet risky, nature of the mid-cap market. Their impressive long-term growth potential makes them worthy of deeper investigation, but the present risks highlight the importance of thorough due diligence. These aren't just buzzworthy stocks; they're case studies in how innovation and adaptation can drive incredible value, even when the broader market feels stagnant.
