Forget the daily price swings and the FUD. A quiet revolution is happening beneath the surface of the Bitcoin market, and it could be signaling something huge: a potential market bottom. Long-term investors are currently holding more Bitcoin than ever before, a move that historically precedes major shifts.

### What Does Record Bitcoin Accumulation by Long-Term Investors Mean?

New data reveals a staggering trend: approximately 16.64 million Bitcoin — roughly 83 percent of the entire circulating supply, valued at an incredible $1.07 trillion — are now held by addresses that haven't moved their holdings for at least 155 days. This isn't just a record; it's a statement.

This massive stash has grown by about 14 percent since November 2025. Why is this significant? Following Bitcoin's all-time high of over $126,000 in October 2025, the market entered a correction phase. But instead of panic selling, the smart money — the long-term believers — saw an opportunity. During this dip, over two million Bitcoin transitioned from short-term speculators to these steadfast, long-term oriented investors.

Historically, such aggressive accumulation by long-term holders, even amidst stagnating or declining prices, has often coincided with the market's bottoming phase. It signals unwavering conviction: capital isn't fleeing the market; it's doubling down, betting on future appreciation. It's a powerful indicator that the strongest hands believe Bitcoin's intrinsic value outweighs any temporary turbulence.

### Why are Long-Term Bitcoin Holders So Confident About Future Gains?

The confidence of these "diamond hands" isn't merely speculative; it’s rooted in market psychology and fundamental belief in Bitcoin's long-term trajectory. When a vast majority of the circulating supply is held tightly, it significantly reduces available sell pressure. This phenomenon creates a more stable foundation, setting the stage for future price rallies once demand inevitably picks up. These investors are essentially removing supply from the active market, creating scarcity that could propel prices higher down the line.

It suggests that despite volatile headlines, a core group of investors believes Bitcoin's revolutionary technology and its role as a decentralized store of value are undeniable. They're playing the long game, looking past immediate fluctuations to the potential of Bitcoin in the global financial landscape.

### Is Bitcoin Nearing its Cycle Low? Expert Predictions and Key Indicators to Watch.

But here's where it gets interesting: not everyone agrees that the definitive market bottom is already in. Zhuoer Jiang, founder of Lubian, offers a more cautious outlook. He predicts the true cycle low might not hit until between October and December 2026, potentially settling in the $42,000-$44,000 range.

Jiang's reasoning hinges on critical indicators like Strategy's mNAV value. This ratio, which compares Bitcoin's market capitalization to the value of held Bitcoin, recently dipped to 0.72. This figure is dangerously close to the May 2022 low of 0.7, suggesting there could be further downside before a definitive reversal occurs. Think of mNAV as a health check for Bitcoin's overall market value relative to its investor base; a lower number often indicates more 'underwater' value, typically preceding a bounce, but it suggests the bottom hasn't been truly tested yet.

So, what's the verdict? Bitcoin's long-term investors are making a bold statement with their record holdings, historically hinting at a market turnaround. Yet, key indicators and expert predictions suggest the ride might not be over, with potential for further consolidation before a true resurgence. One thing is clear: the conviction of Bitcoin's strongest believers is being tested, and their actions speak volumes about where they believe the market is heading next.
