## A Young Boy's Dream: How 10-Year-Old Ejran Wished for Newsround Stardom

In a world often dominated by tough headlines, sometimes a simple, heartwarming story cuts through the noise and reminds us of the power of wishes and the impact of genuine connection. Meet Ejran, a resilient 10-year-old undergoing treatment for a serious heart condition at London's Evelina Children's Hospital. His recent week has been nothing short of extraordinary: a royal encounter, followed by a personal message from the Princess of Wales herself, culminating in a dream come true on national television.

But this wasn't just a chance meeting; it was a moment that sparked a deeper aspiration for Ejran, one that BBC Newsround, the iconic children's news programme, was quick to make a reality.

### The Royal Encounter: Princess Catherine's Visit to Evelina Children's Hospital and a Shared Love for Shrek

The catalyst for Ejran's whirlwind week was a special visit from Catherine, the Princess of Wales. In her role as patron, the Princess was at Evelina Children's Hospital on Monday to tour their new state-of-the-art cardiac unit. During her visit, she spent time chatting with young patients and their families, including Ejran.

Their conversation reportedly touched on the usual hospital routines, but also on something far more relatable and universally loved by children: the animated ogre, Shrek. Sharing a laugh over their mutual appreciation for the green hero, Ejran also revealed his poetic talents to the Princess. This wasn't just a royal walkabout; it was a moment of genuine human connection, a brief respite for a young boy facing significant health challenges.

## Making Wishes Come True: How BBC Newsround Delivered a Message from the Princess

Following his memorable meeting, Ejran shared his ultimate wish with Daily Mail reporter Rebecca English: he desperately hoped his encounter with the Princess would be featured on BBC Newsround. For a child like Ejran, seeing himself on a programme he likely watches regularly isn't just about fame; it's about recognition, a moment of joy, and a connection to the outside world.

And Newsround delivered.

Understanding the significance of such a wish, the programme sent their very own presenter, Ricky Boleto, directly to Ejran's hospital room. What followed was an incredibly poignant moment as Ricky not only confirmed Ejran's appearance on the show but also delivered a personal message straight from Kensington Palace. "The princess was delighted to meet Ejran and looks forward to being updated on his progress," the message read, undoubtedly making Ejran's day.

### Beyond the Broadcast: Why Moments Like Ejran's Newsround Appearance Matter

Ejran's journey from his hospital bed to a national broadcast isn't just a feel-good story; it underscores several crucial points about the power of public figures, media, and the enduring spirit of children.

For Ejran, who regularly misses school due to his ongoing treatment, appearing on Newsround means his friends and classmates can now see him on screen. It transforms a private, challenging experience into a shared moment of pride and celebration. It provides a sense of normalcy and connection when he needs it most.

For Princess Catherine, her visit highlights the impactful role royals play beyond official duties. These personal interactions, particularly with vulnerable children, demonstrate compassion and can genuinely brighten lives, fostering hope and creating lasting memories. It shows that even in the highest echelons, a simple chat about Shrek can forge a meaningful bond.

And for BBC Newsround, this initiative proves the critical role children's media plays in not just informing, but also inspiring and supporting its young audience. By actively fulfilling Ejran's wish, they've shown a deep understanding of what matters to children, turning a news report into a deeply personal and empowering experience.

In a fast-paced news cycle, Ejran's story stands out as a powerful reminder that sometimes, the biggest buzz comes from the smallest, most sincere wishes coming true.
