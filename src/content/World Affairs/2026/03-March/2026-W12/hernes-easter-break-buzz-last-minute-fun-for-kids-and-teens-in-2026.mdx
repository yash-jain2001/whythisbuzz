## Jugendtreff Am Freibad: Creative & Circus Fun for Herne's Youth

The **Jugendtreff Am Freibad (Am Freibad 30)** is a central hub for Easter excitement, catering to both younger children (6-12) and teens (13+). Some activities require pre-registration, so move fast to secure a spot!

### Week 1: Traditional Easter Crafts & Campfire Delights

From **Monday, March 30, to Thursday, April 2**, the first week is all about Spring and Easter vibes. Kids can dive into traditional Easter egg dyeing, crafting, and decorating. The highlight? A cozy campfire with stick bread and an exciting Easter egg hunt on Thursday.

### "Urban Circus": Herne Kids Join the Big Top Show

Running parallel from **9 AM to 3 PM**, the "Urban Circus" project at **Circus Schick Schnack** promises an unforgettable experience. Over four groups, 8- to 14-year-olds will collaborate to create a unique show, blending circus acts, dance, music, and stage design. It's a chance to experience the magic of circus life and perform on stage – transportation from Jugendtreff included! Registration is mandatory.

### Week 2: "Experiment Streetart" – Tagging Walls (Legally!) in Herne

From **Wednesday, April 8, to Friday, April 10**, the "Kulturrucksack" workshop "Experiment Streetart" invites 10- to 14-year-olds to transform a wall on the Jugendtreff Am Freibad grounds. Get ready to leave your mark, legally and artistically! Registration is required.

## Kunsthaus Crange: Unleash the Inner Carpenter at Herne's Wood Workshop

Got a budding builder or a young artisan? The **Kunsthaus Crange (Dorstener Straße 476)** is hosting "Für die Hämmerer" (For the Hammers) wood workshop from **Tuesday, April 7, to Saturday, April 11, 10 AM to 2 PM daily**. Over five days, 9- to 14-year-olds can experiment with various tools and craft their own unique pieces, from small artworks to furniture or even insect hotels. Registration is essential.

## H2Ö: From Baking to Banksy – Diverse Activities for Herne's Young Minds

**H2Ö (Hölkeskampring 2)** offers a packed program for 6- to 12-year-olds, ensuring no two days are the same.

### First Week Wonders: Easter Baskets, Games, & Mystery Trips at H2Ö

The first week is buzzing with activity: crafting Easter baskets, baking delicious cakes, designing carton animals, trying out new board games, dyeing eggs, and a grand egg hunt with prizes. Plus, a surprise excursion is on the cards – details available directly at H2Ö.

### Second Week Spotlight: "Fight For Your Rights" Street Art Workshop for Teens

For 12- to 14-year-olds, the second week features a powerful "Kulturrucksack" workshop: "Fight for your rights." Inspired by artists like Banksy, participants will create stencils to express what truly matters to them, then spray their portrait images onto canvases for a small vernissage. **Registration by April 2 via email is mandatory.**

**Contact H2Ö:**
*   Phone: 02323 164 457
*   Email: beteiligung@herne.de
