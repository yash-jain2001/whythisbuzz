## Immediate Impact: Marl Traffic Gridlocked After Devastating Collision

Marl, Germany – Commuters in Marl woke up to significant disruption today as a severe road accident brought traffic to a standstill on the crucial **Wulfener Straße**. A head-on, or potentially side-impact, collision between a heavy-duty truck and a passenger car has triggered a massive emergency response, turning a routine morning commute into a scene of chaos and urgent rescue efforts.

The incident, occurring sometime this morning, has led to the **complete closure of Wulfener Straße** in both directions. This isn't just a minor fender-bender; eyewitness reports and the sheer scale of the emergency presence suggest a truly devastating impact.
