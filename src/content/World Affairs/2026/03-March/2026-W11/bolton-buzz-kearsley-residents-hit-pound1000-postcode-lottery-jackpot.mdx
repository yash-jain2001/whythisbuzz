## Kearsley Hits the Jackpot: Who Won the Bolton Postcode Lottery Daily Prize?

The excitement is palpable on **Sand Hole Road** and **Glen Avenue** in Kearsley, under the postcode **BL4 8QN**. Players registered at these addresses for the People's Postcode Lottery's daily draw are celebrating a tidy £1,000 prize.

This isn't a one-off anomaly. The People's Postcode Lottery runs 20 individual daily draws, each managed on behalf of different charities. Every day, 20 postcodes are selected, and players in those lucky areas each bag a cool £1,000. It's a simple, yet incredibly effective, way to generate widespread excitement and distribute funds.
