## Costa Cruises Levels Up: Why the 2026/27 Catalog is a Game Changer for Travelers

Get ready to sail into a world of unparalleled Italian flair and breathtaking destinations because **Costa Cruises** has just dropped its highly anticipated 2026/27 catalog. This isn't just a list of trips; it's a 200-plus page manifesto for "La Bella Vita" at sea, packed with **all-inclusive offers**, inspiring new itineraries, and a groundbreaking debut that’s set to redefine winter sun escapes.

From November 2026 through November 2027, Costa is rolling out a diverse range of routes – think sun-drenched **Mediterranean cruises**, exotic **Caribbean getaways**, epic **North European explorations**, and even grand **World Cruises**. But the real buzz? The **Costa Smeralda** is heading to the Canary Islands, bringing a fresh wave of luxury and unique experiences to the Atlantic.

## Unpacking the Value: The All-Inclusive "MyItalian Gourmet Package" Explained

In a move designed to elevate your cruise experience without emptying your wallet, Costa is making an irresistible offer. If you book an all-inclusive dream cruise **by August 3, 2026**, the coveted **MyItalian Gourmet Package** will be included *free of charge* on many departures.

**Why does this matter?** Because this isn't just any drinks package. It's a passport to authentic Italian indulgence:

*   **Premium Beverages:** Enjoy a wide selection of wines, beers, cocktails, coffee, and soft drinks.
*   **Unlimited Gelato:** Indulge in limitless scoops of authentic, homemade Italian gelato.
*   **Neapolitan Pizza Experience:** Savor genuine Neapolitan pizza crafted fresh on board every evening, complete with freshly prepared mozzarella.

This isn't just about saving money; it's about enjoying a truly seamless, worry-free vacation where every culinary desire is met.

## Canary Islands Reimagined: The Costa Smeralda's Atlantic Debut

Say goodbye to winter blues and hello to volcanic landscapes and golden beaches! Starting November 2026, the magnificent **Costa Smeralda**, Costa’s flagship vessel, will embark on a captivating journey through the **Canary Islands and Madeira**.

**What makes this route unique?**

### The Costa Smeralda Experience: Modern Luxury Meets Iconic Itineraries
The Smeralda brings its cutting-edge design, exceptional gastronomic offerings, and vibrant Italian atmosphere to these sunny Atlantic waters. Passengers can expect an immersive journey through a diverse island world, from the lush gardens of Madeira to the dramatic cliffs of Tenerife.

### Tenerife: Two Full Days of Island Discovery
A particular highlight is the extended stay in **Tenerife**, offering two full days to explore the island at a relaxed pace. This longer port call allows guests to delve deeper into the local culture, hike volcanic trails, or simply unwind on stunning beaches – a rare luxury on many cruise itineraries.

For ultimate convenience, Costa is offering its **Fly & Cruise** option, including direct flights from / to Zurich, seamless transfers to the port, and dedicated airport assistance. It's about making your vacation effortless from start to finish.

## Beyond the Horizon: Exclusive "Sea Destinations" You Can't Miss

Costa isn't just taking you to new places; they're creating entirely new experiences *between* destinations with their exclusive "Sea Destinations."

### "Stars of the Sea": A Celestial Spectacle in the Atlantic
Imagine this: between Madeira and Gran Canaria, guests will experience **"Meer der Sterne"** (Stars of the Sea) – a spectacular stargazing event in one of the darkest spots of the Atlantic. It’s a magical moment where the heavens meet the ocean, offering an unparalleled view of the cosmos.

### "Atlantis Crest": Diving into Myth and Mystery
Between Lanzarote and Tenerife, prepare to be captivated by **"Atlantis Crest."** Inspired by the legendary myth of Atlantis, this experience delves into a mysterious underwater world, blending storytelling with unique onboard activities. These aren't just moments; they're memories crafted exclusively by Costa.
