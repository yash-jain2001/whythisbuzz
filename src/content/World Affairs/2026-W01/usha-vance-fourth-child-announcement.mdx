US Vice President **JD Vance** and **Second Lady Usha Vance** have announced that they are expecting their **fourth child**, a baby boy, due in **July**.

The couple shared the news through a joint social media post, offering a rare personal update from a family that usually maintains a relatively low public profile.

---

## What the Announcement Said

In their statement, the Vances said they were excited to welcome another child into their family and confirmed that both Usha Vance and the baby are healthy.

They also expressed gratitude toward the medical professionals and staff who support their family, particularly those who help balance public service responsibilities with family life.

The message was widely shared online and prompted congratulatory responses from supporters and well-wishers.

JD and Usha Vance are already parents to **three children**:

## Who Is Usha Vance?

Before becoming Second Lady, **Usha Vance** built a respected legal career. She previously worked at **Munger, Tolles & Olson LLP** and served as a law clerk for senior members of the US judiciary, including **Chief Justice John Roberts** and **Brett Kavanaugh** during his time on the federal appeals court.

She met JD Vance while studying at **Yale Law School**, and the couple married in **2014**.
