The era of "too many apps" is ending. We are returning to the cable bundle, just over the internet.

## The Big Mergers

Paramount and Peacock joining forces was just the start. The economics of streaming only work at massive scale, forcing consolidation.

## What It Means for You

- **Higher Prices**: Less competition means higher subscription fees.
- **Fewer Shows**: The "Peak TV" era of 500 scripted shows a year is over. Expect fewer, safer bets.
