## Kevin Costner and Jake Gyllenhaal Head Down Under for Amazon MGM's "Honeymoon with Harry"

Get ready for an unlikely duo to light up the screen! Hollywood titans Kevin Costner and Jake Gyllenhaal are teaming up for the Amazon MGM Studios dramedy, *Honeymoon with Harry*. Set to kick off filming in Australia's breathtaking Queensland this April, this production is already generating significant buzz – and for good reason. Not only does it promise a compelling narrative rooted in an award-winning novel, but it also marks a continued strategic investment in Australian production by one of the biggest players in streaming and cinema.

### What is the Plot of "Honeymoon with Harry" Starring Kevin Costner and Jake Gyllenhaal?

Based on Bart Baker’s novel, *Honeymoon with Harry* throws a curveball at traditional romantic comedy tropes. The story centers on a "rough-around-the-edges" man, portrayed by Jake Gyllenhaal, whose meticulously planned future takes an unexpected turn. Instead of a blissful honeymoon with his fiancée, he finds himself on an unwanted journey with her "prickly, overprotective" father, played by the legendary Kevin Costner.

Picture this: two vastly different men, forced into close quarters on a tropical island honeymoon that's anything but romantic. The initial clash and bickering are inevitable, setting the stage for some classic dramedy gold. But beneath the surface, the narrative promises a gradual uncovering of an "unexpected bond," exploring themes of loss, connection, and the surprising ways relationships can evolve. It's a setup ripe for both heartfelt moments and genuine laughs, showcasing the nuanced acting these two stars are known for.

### Who Else is in the "Honeymoon with Harry" Cast and Crew?

Beyond the marquee names, the film is in good hands. Directors Glenn Ficarra and John Requa, known for their work on projects like *Crazy, Stupid, Love.* and *This Is Us*, bring a proven track record of blending comedy with genuine emotional depth – a perfect fit for this dramedy. Adding to the talent roster, Sarah Pidgeon, recognized for her role in *I Know What You Did Last Summer*, is currently in talks to join the cast, hinting at an ensemble that could truly elevate the story.

This combination of experienced directors and a stellar cast, especially with the intriguing dynamic between Costner and Gyllenhaal, positions *Honeymoon with Harry* as a film to watch.

### Why is Amazon MGM Filming "Honeymoon with Harry" in Queensland, Australia?

The choice of Queensland, Australia, isn't just about stunning visuals – though the tropical islands of The Whitsundays and the urban charm of Brisbane will undoubtedly provide a gorgeous backdrop. This decision is a strategic one, reflecting Amazon MGM Studios' growing reliance on Queensland as a production hub. It's part of a "steady stream" of projects for the studio in the state, driven by competitive incentives offered by Screen Queensland's Production Attraction Strategy (PAS).

Minister for the Arts, John-Paul Langbroek, highlighted the significant economic windfall, estimating that *Honeymoon with Harry* will inject **an estimated $51 million into the state’s economy and create 215 jobs**. This isn't just about making movies; it's about boosting local economies, nurturing talent, and showcasing Queensland as a global tourism destination. Screen Queensland CEO Jacqui Feeney underscored this partnership, praising the local screen industry's "high-quality expertise" and the state's "captivating locations and first-class infrastructure."

### The Bigger Picture: What Does This Mean for Amazon MGM and International Production?

The ongoing investment by a giant like Amazon MGM in international locations like Queensland signals a larger trend in the film industry. Studios are increasingly looking globally for skilled crews, diverse locations, and attractive financial incentives. For Amazon, this strategy helps them deliver a vast slate of original content to their global Prime Video subscribers while optimizing production costs and fostering international relationships.

*Honeymoon with Harry* isn't just another movie; it's a testament to the power of global collaboration in filmmaking. It underscores how major studios are leveraging international partnerships to create compelling content, boost local economies, and ultimately, bring diverse stories to audiences worldwide.

## The Bottom Line: Why This Buzz Matters for Movie Fans and the Industry

With two powerhouse actors, a talented directing duo, a unique premise, and the stunning visuals of Queensland, *Honeymoon with Harry* has all the ingredients to be a standout dramedy. For fans, it's a chance to see Costner and Gyllenhaal in a fresh, unexpected dynamic. For the industry, it's a clear signal of Amazon MGM's commitment to strategic international production and the growing strength of Australia's film sector.
