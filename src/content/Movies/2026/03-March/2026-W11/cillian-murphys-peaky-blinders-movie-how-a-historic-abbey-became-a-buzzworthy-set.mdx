## From Stately Home to Screen Star: Cillian Murphy & Peaky Blinders Invade Calke Abbey

The whispers were true, and the buzz has now exploded. Derbyshire's historic Calke Abbey, usually a quiet testament to the decline of the country house, was recently thrust into the cinematic limelight, transformed into a pivotal backdrop for the highly anticipated film, *Peaky Blinders: The Immortal Man*. Starring Oscar-winner Cillian Murphy as the iconic Tommy Shelby, the film's production wasn't just a fleeting visit; it was a game-changer, breathing new life and crucial funds into the revered National Trust property.

Released in cinemas this past Friday and set to hit Netflix on March 20th, the movie brought Hollywood-level magic (and meticulous detail) to the stately home. For two months in late 2024, Calke Abbey became a bustling hub of activity, a stark contrast to its typical serene atmosphere, marking the *first-ever* drama filmed on its grounds.

### How Calke Abbey Was Transformed for Peaky Blinders: The Immortal Man

"They transformed these spaces in an incredible way," recounted Caroline Taylor, Calke Abbey's visitor operations manager, still visibly impressed by the scale of the operation. The production team masterfully repurposed the Abbey to reflect the film's 1940 setting, set against the grim reality of the Birmingham Blitz during World War Two. Imagine artificial snow blanketing the grounds and period-specific dressings replacing centuries of history – it was a monumental undertaking that essentially reset Calke Abbey's aesthetic for the cameras.

The challenge wasn't just aesthetic; it was logistical. Over 1,300 delicate objects and artifacts had to be carefully packed away, ensuring the preservation of the Abbey's true heritage while making way for cinematic artifice.

### Behind the Scenes: The Massive Scale of Peaky Blinders Film Production

This wasn't just a few actors and a camera. Taylor described the filming as a "whole enterprise," a beehive of activity involving hundreds of professionals. Beyond Cillian Murphy, the film features a stellar cast including Rebecca Ferguson and Packy Lee, but it was the vast behind-the-scenes machinery that truly defined the experience. Make-up artists, hair stylists, costume designers, caterers, lighting crews – an entire mini-economy descended upon the Abbey.

While the cast, including Murphy, remained intensely focused and in character throughout the shoot (limiting social interaction with staff), the sheer presence of such a high-profile production brought an undeniable energy. Even as the Abbey remained open to visitors, staff were sworn to secrecy, adding an extra layer of intrigue and anticipation to the everyday operations.

## Why Peaky Blinders Filming is a Vital Lifeline for Calke Abbey and the National Trust

Beyond the glitz and glamour, the decision to host *Peaky Blinders: The Immortal Man* was critically important for Calke Abbey's long-term sustainability. General Manager Stuart Alcock emphasized the financial injection as "critically important" for the National Trust, which operates as a charity.

### Boosting Heritage Preservation Through Blockbuster Cinema

"The National Trust is a charity and cares for Calke and is reliant on raising the funds to do that," Alcock explained. "It is extremely expensive looking after these places." The income generated from the filming fees provides a vital lifeline, helping to cover the immense costs of maintaining a historic property of Calke Abbey's magnitude. But the benefits extend beyond direct revenue. The film offers unparalleled exposure, sharing the beauty and unique story of Calke Abbey with a global audience who might never have otherwise discovered it. This newfound visibility is expected to attract a significant boost in visitor numbers, creating a ripple effect of economic benefit.

## The Future is Bright: Calke Abbey's Post-Peaky Blinders Buzz

As Calke Abbey staff eagerly await the full movie – "we are just fit to burst in terms of really wanting to see the full movie," Alcock admitted – the long-term impact of this cinematic adventure is clear. By embracing the world of *Peaky Blinders* and Cillian Murphy's compelling performance, Calke Abbey has not only secured crucial funding but has also cemented its place in pop culture, promising a future rich with new visitors and renewed interest in its own fascinating history.

Catch *Peaky Blinders: The Immortal Man* in cinemas now, and streaming globally on Netflix from March 20th.
