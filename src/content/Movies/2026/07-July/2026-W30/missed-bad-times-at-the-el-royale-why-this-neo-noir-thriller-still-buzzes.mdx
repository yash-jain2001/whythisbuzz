## Unpacking the El Royale's Dark Secrets: What's the Plot?

For the uninitiated, "Bad Times at the El Royale" isn't just a catchy title; it's a promise. The film plunges us into the dilapidated grandeur of the El Royale, a forgotten hotel straddling the California-Nevada border. Here, seven strangers converge, each harboring their own deep secrets and hidden agendas.

We meet a charismatic but secretive priest (played by the incomparable **Jeff Bridges**), a soulful singer struggling for a comeback (**Cynthia Erivo**), a slick vacuum cleaner salesman (**Jon Hamm**), and a mysterious, nameless traveler. As they check into their eerily similar rooms, they quickly discover that the hotel isn't just run-down; it's rigged. Every room is under surveillance, and beneath the veneer of hospitality, a sinister game is afoot. This isn't just a place to rest your head; it's a crucible where pasts collide, and no one is who they seem.
