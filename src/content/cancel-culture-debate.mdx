The term "Cancel Culture" has become so loaded it's almost meaningless. But the mechanisms of social accountability are real.

## Side A: Accountability

Proponents argue that "cancellation" is simply the free market of specific ideas reacting to behavior. It's about consequences for actions that were previously ignored.

## Side B: Mob Mentality

Critics argue that the punishment often outweighs the crime, with no path for redemption. The speed of social media judgment allows for no nuance or context.

## The Middle Ground

Most people support accountability but reject permanent excommunication for minor missteps. The conversation is shifting towards "Restorative Justice" rather than just punishment.
